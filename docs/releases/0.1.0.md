The first release of iWeather Card.

- **Animated sky** behind the card, drawn from your weather entity and the sun's position: clouds, rain, snow, fog, lightning, stars and the moon in its current phase.
- **Any size**, from a one-line strip to a full page with alerts, hourly and daily forecasts, a weather map and 12 detail tiles. Tap a tile for its charts and a comparison with yesterday.
- **Weather map** with radar, a 12-hour precipitation forecast, wind, temperature and air quality.
- **Works with any weather integration.** Any reading can come from a sensor instead, and readings your weather entity leaves out can come from a second weather entity or Open-Meteo.
- **Visual editor** for every option, with presets and undo.
- **17 languages**, following your Home Assistant language.

## Install

Needs Home Assistant 2023.9.3 or newer.

- **HACS:** use the **Open in HACS** button in the README, or add `https://github.com/sitapix/iweather-card` as a custom repository with the type **Dashboard**. Install **iWeather Card**, then reload your browser.
- **By hand:** download every file attached to this release into one folder, such as `/config/www/iweather/`, and add `/local/iweather/iweather-card.js` as a **JavaScript module** under **Settings → Dashboards → ⋮ → Resources**.
